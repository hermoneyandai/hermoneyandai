Fermented Ragi Idli (Flight-Ready)
Live

What I made instead of the airline's infant meal — soft, familiar, and easy for baby to eat with her hands mid-flight.

Ingredients
  • 2 spoons fermented ragi powder
  • 2 spoons sooji (semolina)
  • 2 spoons poha, soaked 5 minutes and ground
  • 1 spoon any baby masala
  • 2 spoons curd (plus 2 more later)
  • Water, as needed for consistency
  • Ghee, for coating the idli maker
Method
  1. Mix the ragi powder, sooji, ground poha, baby masala, and 2 spoons curd well.
  2. Let the batter rest for 10 minutes.
  3. Add 2 more spoons of curd and enough water to reach a smooth, pourable consistency.
  4. Coat the idli maker with ghee.
  5. Add the batter into the moulds.
  6. Steam for 15 minutes.
  7. Let rest for 2–3 minutes before removing.
  8. Take the idlis out.
Travel tip: pack in silver foil, then into a zip lock bag — keeps things light and mess-free for a flight bag.
