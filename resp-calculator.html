💡
CESG grant tip
The government matches 20% on the first $2,500/year = max $500/year per child. Lifetime max is $7,200. Contributing $208/month maximises your grant every year.
What if they don't go to school?
✅
Your contributions — always yours
You get back every dollar you put in. No tax. No penalty. This money was already taxed before you contributed.
⚠️
Government grants — returned
All CESG grants must be returned to the government. You keep nothing from the grants if the RESP isn't used for education.
💸
Investment growth — taxed + penalty
Growth is called an Accumulated Income Payment (AIP). Taxed at your marginal rate plus an extra 20% penalty. This is the painful part.
🛡️
RRSP rollover — avoid the penalty
If you have RRSP room, you can roll up to $50,000 of AIP into your RRSP — no penalty, tax deferred to retirement. Conditions: RESP open 10+ years, child 21+ and not in school.
Also remember: RESP can be used for trade school, college, apprenticeships and some programs abroad — not just university. You have until the RESP's 35th year to use the funds.
