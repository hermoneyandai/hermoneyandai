First Week Home

Register your baby's birth

Most urgent item 14–30 days depending on province Free

This is the most time-sensitive item on this list. Deadlines vary by province — New Brunswick is 14 days, Manitoba is 5 days, most others are 30 days. Miss it and you're looking at extra steps and delays for everything else (SIN, CCB, passport).

Most hospitals will give you a registration package before discharge. Complete it and submit it to your province's vital statistics office.

💡 Kirti's tip: Do this before you leave the hospital if you can. Everything else — SIN, CCB, OHIP — flows from this one step.
Ontario British Columbia Alberta Quebec New Brunswick Nova Scotia Manitoba Saskatchewan Newfoundland PEI

Apply for your baby's SIN

Free Right after birth registration

You need your baby's SIN to open their RESP and apply for CCB. Apply online through Service Canada — free, takes about 10 business days to arrive by mail.

Alberta: birth registration, SIN, and CCB are all bundled — easiest process in the country.

💡 Kirti's tip: Don't wait for the physical SIN card before opening the RESP. The number is enough — you'll get it in your confirmation letter.
Apply for SIN — Canada.ca

Register your baby for provincial health insurance

Not automatic in most provinces Free

In most provinces this is NOT automatic — you need to register separately. Your baby's first doctor visit (usually 3–5 days after discharge) will ask for health card info.

Quebec: RAMQ is automatic if a parent is already covered.

💡 Kirti's tip: Do this the same week as birth registration so your baby is covered before their first checkup.
Ontario OHIP BC MSP Alberta AHCIP Manitoba Health

First Month — Money & Benefits

Apply for Canada Child Benefit (CCB)

Don't miss this money Up to $7,997/year

The government gives you up to $7,997/year per child under 6 (2025–2026 rates). This is not automatic — you have to apply through CRA My Account or by mailing form RC66.

Apply as soon as your baby is born. Payments start the month after your application is processed.

💡 Kirti's tip: File your taxes every year even if you had no income on mat leave. CCB is income-tested — CRA uses your tax return. If you don't file, payments stop.
CCB — Canada.ca

Apply for EI Maternity & Parental Benefits

Apply within 4 weeks of last day of work Up to $729/week in 2026

Apply within 4 weeks of your last day of work. You need 600 insurable hours to qualify. EI pays 55% of your insurable earnings up to $729/week in 2026.

Maternity: 15 weeks (birth parent only). Standard parental: 40 weeks total. Extended parental: 69 weeks total at 33% of earnings. Always apply maternity and parental together.

💡 Kirti's tip: Set aside 10–15% of every EI payment for taxes. EI is taxable income and CRA does not withhold enough automatically. You could owe at tax time.
Apply for EI — Canada.ca EI Calculator

Open your baby's RESP

$500/year free government grant Open before Dec 31 of birth year

The government matches 20% of your first $2,500 contributed each year — that's $500 in free money per year through the Canada Education Savings Grant (CESG). Lifetime max is $7,200 in grants.

You need your baby's SIN to open the account. Even a $100 contribution in year one locks in that year's grant eligibility.

Travel Documents

Apply for your baby's Canadian passport

$58.50 as of March 2026 Apply 3 months before travel

Child passports are $58.50 (as of March 31, 2026 — up from $57), valid for 5 years. You'll also need a passport photo. Processing takes up to 20 business days by mail.

Mailing tip: Use Canada Post Registered Mail ($9.75 + postage) — you get tracking and proof of mailing. You do NOT need to use a courier.

💡 Kirti's tip: I sent mine via Purolator and overpaid. Canada Post Registered Mail does the exact same job for a fraction of the cost. Don't make my mistake.
Child Passport — Canada.ca Canada Post Registered Mail

Apply for OCI Card (Indian-origin babies)

Fee applies 8–12 weeks processing If applicable

If your baby is of Indian origin and holds Canadian citizenship, they are eligible for an OCI card — visa-free travel to India for life. Processing takes 8–12 weeks.

You need your baby's Canadian passport first before applying for OCI. Apply through BLS India Canada — NOT VFS Global (that's the US portal).

💡 Kirti's tip: Start early — 8–12 weeks processing means plan well ahead of any India travel. Passport first, OCI second.

First 6 Months

Add your baby to daycare waitlists

Do this NOW Waitlists are 1–3 years in most cities

In Ontario, subsidized $10/day daycare waitlists can be 1 to 3 years long. You can register before your baby is even born in some municipalities.

In Ontario, register on the Ontario Child Care Portal. You can apply to multiple daycares at once. No cost to join the waitlist.

💡 Kirti's tip: Put your name on every waitlist within a reasonable distance — you can always say no when a spot opens. The cost of NOT being on the list is losing years of $10/day care.
Ontario Child Care Portal

Update your life insurance beneficiary

Almost always forgotten

Your beneficiary does NOT automatically update when you have a baby. Contact your HR or insurance provider and update it manually. Also review your will if you have one.

💡 Kirti's tip: This takes 10 minutes and most people never do it. If something happens to you, this determines where the money goes.

Track your baby's vaccine schedule

All routine vaccines are free 2 months · 4 months · 6 months · 12 months · 18 months

All routine childhood vaccines are free in Canada through your provincial health program. First vaccines are at 2 months. Your doctor will track these but knowing the schedule helps you stay on top of bookings.
